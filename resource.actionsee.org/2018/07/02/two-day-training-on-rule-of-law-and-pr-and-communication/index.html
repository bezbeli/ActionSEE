Two Day Training on Rule of Law and PR and Communication

July 2, 2018

Blerina Ramaj

Kosovo grantee from the ActionSEE project, Youth Association for Human Rights YAHR from Lipjan, has taken part in a two-day training from 28-29 of June, focused on Rule of Law and on PR and Communication organized by Open Data Kosovo


Youth Association for Human Rights YAHR is a non-governmental organization which seeks to create a democratic, and tolerant platform where human rights are respected. YAHR’s mission is promotion, protection and identification of human rights violations mainly through youth activities.

NGO YAHR within the funded project from ActionSEE is working towards strengthening of local media through the formation of operational mechanisms in order to increase the transparency and accountability of local institutions. These operational mechanisms will be used as a tool to measure and eventually increase the openness index, which is one of the core goals of ActionSEE.
