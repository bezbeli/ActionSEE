Knowledge exchange workshop with grantees from North Macedonia and Kosovo, accompanied by GOT Data Conference in Kosovo

December 5, 2019

goce

On December 5th, a Knowledge exchange workshop was hosted by Open Data Kosovo in Prishtina, where ACTION SEE grantees from North Macedonia and Kosovo talked about their experiences, challenges and lessons learnt during the implementation of their grants.

As part of the knowledge exchange, the participants had the opportunity to also attend the GOT Data Conference which was held between 05-06.12.2019.

 

The Conference is focused on Open Contracting, Artificial Intelligence and Policy-Making, Data Journalism, as well as models of collaboration between implementing partners.

More information on the Conference and the speakers can be found on the following link: https://got-data.org/
