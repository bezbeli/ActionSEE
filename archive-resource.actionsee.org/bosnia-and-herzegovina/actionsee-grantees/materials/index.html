Association for Media and Civil Society Development (URMCD) & Perspektiva.plus – FAKAT TV Show

 

By producing 5 episode miniseries to identify misleading narratives in popular media and politics, map financial and political motivations for their existence and to find key actors in their production this project aims to help make political accountability a key expectation of the general public in the electoral process of Bosnia and Herzegovina, and to raise general public’s expectation toward media platforms in terms of quality and reliability of the cited sources

The organization Centre for Policy and Governance shall implement “Consistency of the budget with reforms”

 

The overall objective of this project is to enhance the role of civil society and media during the pre-election period by providing objective information on the commitment of authorities to the socioeconomic reforms.

Research on the consistency of budgets with reforms (identifying the most important weak spots of 2018 budgets which are not in line with the socioeconomic reforms), advocacy campaign (writing analytical articles in partnership with one of the leading online news portals in the country – BUKA and managing a social media campaign).

The organization UAMPI BUM (Association for Alternative Music Production and Publishing BOOM)  in collaboration with UNSA Geto (Association for Independent Creators and Activists GETO) and Impuls Portal – “Preglasaj” project

Preglasaj is a project that produced textual works and video reports which speak about real problems young people face and their lives. The content is created by students and activists under the guidance of experienced journalists from Bosnia and Herzegovina and our region.
