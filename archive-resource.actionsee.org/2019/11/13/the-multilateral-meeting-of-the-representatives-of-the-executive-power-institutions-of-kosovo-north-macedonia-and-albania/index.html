The Multilateral Meeting of the Representatives of the Executive Power Institutions of Kosovo, North Macedonia and Albania

November 13, 2019

Blerina Ramaj

The Multilateral Meeting of the Representatives of the Executive Power Institutions of Kosovo, North Macedonia and Albania part of the regional Accountability, Technology and Institutional Openness Network in SEE Project unfolded with a success today!

Open Data Kosovo in close collaboration with Westminster Foundation for Democracy / Western Balkans Democracy Initiative were happy to welcome the participants, where the partners from North Macedonia – Metamorphosis Foundation, and Albania- Lëvizja MJAFT presented the work and achievements in promoting institutional transparency.

Moreover, representatives from the Office of Prime Minister and Ministries from Kosovo, North Macedonia and Albania shared the initiatives in regard to openness aiming to share peer-learning.

Many interesting and thoughtful insights were exchanged during the meeting and overall the three countries have all improved through years on their institutional openness and transparency, thus enabling more accountability.
