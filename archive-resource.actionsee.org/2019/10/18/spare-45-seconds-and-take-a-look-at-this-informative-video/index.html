Grantee Media Pulse video; Importance of citizen actions to mitigate democratization challenges

October 18, 2019

Blerina Ramaj

The third grantee Association for Capacity Improvement “Media Pulse” as part of the ‘ActionSEE’ Project has put together this amazing video that portrays the importance of citizen actions to mitigate democratization challenges.

In a country where democracy prevails, it is the power and responsibility of us the citizens, to push forward necessary changes to create a transparent and citizen-friendly environment.
