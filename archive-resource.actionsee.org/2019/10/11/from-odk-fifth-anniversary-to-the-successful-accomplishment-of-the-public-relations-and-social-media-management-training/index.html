Open Data Kosovo holds training of Public Relations and Social Media Management

October 11, 2019

Blerina Ramaj

The two day public relations and social media management training ended in success. As part of the Accountability, Technology and Institutional Openness Network in SEE Project, participants coming from the third grantee from Leposavic, Association for Capacity Improvement “Media Pulse”, received a training on social media management. The participants were informed on the originality, simplicity, and consistency of outreach activities. Furthermore, defining the audience and reaching the right audience were two components elaborated in the workshop.

Open Data Kosovo would like to thank Benjamin Kolenović from Hallakate team for the clear explanation and elaboration of key components that shape a campaign into an effective one by spreading out the important message that specific campaign entails.
